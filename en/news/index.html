My research experience covers computer vision, mobile RGB-D measurement, hydrological modelling, multimodal reasoning, indoor localisation and cardiac image segmentation.ResearchMapAgent: Indoor Localisation and Spatial ReasoningA Qwen3-based localisation agent that selects candidate positions using BLE, motion and map evidence, with independent verification and deterministic fallback.ResearchMetroHeight: Mobile RGB-D Cargo Height EstimationAn iOS capture app and FastAPI backend that estimate trolley cargo height from smartphone RGB-D captures in metro stations.ResearchHydrological Constraints on Post-Fire Vegetation RecoveryAn analysis of modelled soil water and MODIS NDVI using the 2019–2020 Gospers Mountain megafire as a natural experiment.ResearchSpatiotemporal Flood Forecasting and Hydrological ModellingA hydrological modelling project combining river-network structure, gauge observations and reanalysis data to study river-discharge forecasting.ResearchCardiac Segmentation and Spatiotemporal ModellingA study of temporal recalibration and cyclic trajectory regularisation for 2D+t cine MRI segmentation under ED/ES-only supervision.ResearchVisual Metaphor and Multimodal ReasoningAn 800-image benchmark and a dual-block VLM-agent framework for visual-metaphor reasoning and generation in multi-agent communication games.ResearchResearch and Development of Third-Generation Intelligent Arrow Selection Equipment and SystemsA multimodal research workflow connecting arrow measurements, high-speed video and target images to assess shot dispersion and stability across rounds.
