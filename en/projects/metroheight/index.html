MetroHeight: Mobile RGB-D Cargo Height Estimation

An iOS capture app and FastAPI backend that estimate trolley cargo height from smartphone RGB-D captures in metro stations.

Research Background

Cargo-height inspection in metro stations requires a physical measurement from the local floor to the top of the load. A handheld RGB-D image contains the cargo, trolley structures and surrounding scene, so the measurement pipeline must identify which geometry belongs to the load and establish a reliable floor reference.

MetroHeight connects an iOS capture application with a FastAPI analysis backend. The research covers the complete measurement process, from camera and depth alignment to segmentation, point-cloud processing and height estimation, using smartphone RGB-D data captured in the station environment.

Research Question

How can a handheld RGB-D capture produce a physical floor-to-cargo height measurement while separating the load from the trolley and surrounding scene?

Individual Contributions

  • Built the iOS capture app and FastAPI backend for cargo-height inspection of delivery trolleys in metro stations.
  • Aligned camera intrinsics to the depth-map resolution and filtered invalid and low-confidence cargo depth samples.
  • Integrated SAM 3 segmentation and DBSCAN clustering to isolate cargo geometry.
  • Implemented gravity-aligned 3D reconstruction, local RANSAC floor fitting and robust cargo-top estimation.

Data & Study Design

The evaluation comprises 194 capture records across four trolley categories at Hong Kong MTR’s Wong Tai Sin Station. Laser measurements provide the height reference. The evaluated implementation uses RGB-D captures and orientation information from an iPhone 14 Pro Max.

Each record is processed five times and the mean output is compared with its laser reference. These runs reuse the same capture: the dataset remains 194 capture records. The reported 3.13 cm MAE and 4.81 cm RMSE use this record-level averaging protocol.

Research Methods

  1. 01Capture RGB, depth and orientation information in the iOS app, then submit the capture to the FastAPI analysis backend.
  2. 02Map the segmentation and camera intrinsics to the depth-map resolution. Retain valid, sufficiently confident cargo-depth samples and select the largest non-noise cargo point-cloud cluster with DBSCAN.
  3. 03Use SAM 3 segmentation and gravity-aligned 3D reconstruction to separate the cargo geometry from the surrounding scene.
  4. 04Fit a local floor plane with RANSAC and estimate the cargo top relative to that plane, excluding trolley structures from the load-height measurement.

Evaluation & Findings

3.13 cmMean absolute error

194Capture records · 4 categories

Evaluated 194 capture records across four trolley categories at Hong Kong MTR’s Wong Tai Sin Station against laser reference measurements. The system achieved 3.13 cm MAE and 4.81 cm RMSE using the mean of five processing runs per capture.

Discussion

The measurement combines several dependent stages: object segmentation, depth-quality selection, coordinate alignment and floor estimation. The evaluation therefore concerns the complete pipeline and its specified capture protocol; repeated computation measures variation in the processing of an existing observation.
