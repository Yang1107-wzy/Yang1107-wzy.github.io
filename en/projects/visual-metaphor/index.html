Visual Metaphor and Multimodal Reasoning

An 800-image benchmark and a dual-block VLM-agent framework for visual-metaphor reasoning and generation in multi-agent communication games.

Research Background

Visual metaphors require an agent to connect image content with meanings that are not stated directly. In communication games, that interpretation also depends on what teammates and opponents may know, and on the objective of the current interaction.

The project combines MetaVision-DB, a visual-semantic benchmark, with MACH, a dual-block vision-language-agent framework. The research studies both the interpretation of visual evidence and the generation of expressions conditioned on a communication strategy.

Research Question

How can an agent interpret indirect visual meanings, infer another player’s knowledge and choose expressions that serve a communication strategy?

Individual Contributions

  • Led MetaVision-DB data construction across 800 images, four visual–semantic relations and three communication games.
  • Organised concept–image alignment, schema validation and manual quality review.
  • Co-developed MACH with contrastive visual-semantic reasoning and strategy-conditioned metaphor generation.
  • Evaluated MACH in Visual Undercover under explicit civilian and undercover team configurations.

Data & Study Design

The benchmark contains 800 images spanning four visual–semantic relations and three communication games: Visual Undercover, Visual Taboo and Visual Dixit. Data construction includes concept–image alignment, schema validation and manual quality review.

The reported 97.2% result uses Gemini 3.1 Pro in Visual Undercover, with three MACH civilian agents playing against two Raw-VLM undercover agents. It is the civilian team’s win rate for this specified game and team configuration, rather than a general image-understanding accuracy.

Research Methods

  1. 01Organise concept–image examples across the three games and four visual–semantic relations, with consistent schemas and manual review.
  2. 02Use contrastive visual-semantic reasoning to interpret an image and compare hypotheses about its meaning in the game context.
  3. 03Condition metaphor generation on communication strategy, supported by structured memory and theory-derived metaphor knowledge.
  4. 04Simulate teammate and opponent responses when evaluating candidate expressions and selecting a communication action.

Evaluation & Findings

In Visual Undercover using Gemini 3.1 Pro, three MACH civilian agents achieved a 97.2% win rate against two Raw-VLM undercover agents. This is the result for that specific game and team configuration.

Discussion

The evaluation links model behaviour to a particular communication objective, backbone and team assignment. Interpreting a game win rate therefore requires the corresponding interaction rules and experimental configuration, alongside the quality of the underlying visual-semantic data.
