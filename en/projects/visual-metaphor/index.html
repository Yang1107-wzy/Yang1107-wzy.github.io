Visual Metaphor and Multimodal Reasoning

An 800-image benchmark and a dual-block VLM-agent framework for visual-metaphor reasoning and generation in multi-agent communication games.

The research question

How can an agent interpret indirect visual meanings, infer another player’s knowledge and choose expressions that serve a communication strategy?

My contribution

  1. Led MetaVision-DB data construction across 800 images, four visual–semantic relations and three communication games.
  2. Organised concept–image alignment, schema validation and manual quality review.
  3. Co-developed MACH with contrastive visual-semantic reasoning and strategy-conditioned metaphor generation.
  4. Evaluated MACH in Visual Undercover under explicit civilian and undercover team configurations.

How it works

  1. 01Use Visual Undercover, Visual Taboo and Visual Dixit to study different forms of visual communication.
  2. 02Separate evidence interpretation from strategy-conditioned expression and action generation.
  3. 03Connect the two blocks through structured memory and theory-derived metaphor knowledge.
  4. 04Simulate teammate and opponent responses when selecting candidate expressions.

Evaluation & findings

800Images · MetaVision-DB
3Communication games
97.2%Win rate · specified 3-vs-2 setting

In Visual Undercover using Gemini 3.1 Pro, three MACH civilian agents achieved a 97.2% win rate against two Raw-VLM undercover agents. This is the result for that specific game and team configuration.

Visual communication depends on the relationship between what an image depicts, what an expression suggests and what other agents know. The game objective and team configuration shape how a win rate should be interpreted.
