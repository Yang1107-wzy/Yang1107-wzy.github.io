Overview

Improve the representation of river dynamics in regional flood-forecasting research.

Selected contributions

  • Developed a forecasting approach that represents river-network relationships.
  • Integrated reanalysis, hydrometric observations and geospatial data covering 75 gauges across 15 rivers.
  • Implemented and assessed models for hydrological time-series prediction.

Research status

Second author of “A Spatiotemporal Hybrid Framework for Regional Flood Forecasting”; abstract accepted for poster presentation at WRE 2026. Research under Guangdong Major Project of Basic and Applied Basic Research No. 2021B0301030007, “High-Resolution Simulation and Prediction of the Land Surface Environment”.
