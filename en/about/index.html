AboutAbout Zhengyang WangI am a Computer Science and Technology undergraduate at Beijing Normal-Hong Kong Baptist University, with expected graduation in June 2027. My research experience covers computer vision, mobile RGB-D measurement, hydrological modelling, multimodal reasoning, indoor localisation and cardiac image segmentation.ResearchMapAgent: Indoor Localisation and Spatial ReasoningMapAgent is an ongoing final year project exploring language-model agents for indoor localisation and spatial reasoning.ResearchMetroHeight: Mobile RGB-D Cargo Height EstimationMetroHeight explores smartphone RGB-D sensing for cargo-height measurement on delivery trolleys in metro stations.ResearchHydrological Constraints on Post-Fire Vegetation RecoveryThis project examines how soil-water conditions relate to vegetation recovery after the Gospers Mountain megafire.ResearchSpatiotemporal Flood Forecasting and Hydrological ModellingThis project studies regional river-discharge forecasting through hydrological observations and spatial information.ResearchCardiac Segmentation and Spatiotemporal ModellingST-Net studies cardiac cine MRI segmentation using temporal information when segmentation labels are limited to selected cardiac phases.ResearchVisual Metaphor and Multimodal ReasoningThis project studies visual metaphor generation and reasoning in multi-agent communication games through MetaVision-DB and MACH.ResearchResearch and Development of Third-Generation Intelligent Arrow Selection Equipment and SystemsData-driven arrow selection connects visual measurements with shooting performance.
