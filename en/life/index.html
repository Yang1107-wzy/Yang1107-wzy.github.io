AboutAbout Zhengyang WangI am a Computer Science and Technology undergraduate at Beijing Normal-Hong Kong Baptist University, with expected graduation in June 2027. My research experience covers computer vision, mobile RGB-D measurement, hydrological modelling, multimodal reasoning, indoor localisation and cardiac image segmentation.ResearchResearch and Development of Third-Generation Intelligent Arrow Selection Equipment and SystemsA multimodal research workflow connecting arrow measurements, high-speed video and target images to assess shot dispersion and stability across rounds.ResearchMetroHeight: Mobile RGB-D Cargo Height EstimationAn iOS capture app and FastAPI backend that estimate trolley cargo height from smartphone RGB-D captures in metro stations.ResearchSpatiotemporal Flood Forecasting and Hydrological ModellingA hydrological modelling project combining river-network structure, gauge observations and reanalysis data to study river-discharge forecasting.ResearchVisual Metaphor and Multimodal ReasoningAn 800-image benchmark and a dual-block VLM-agent framework for visual-metaphor reasoning and generation in multi-agent communication games.ResearchMapAgent: Indoor Localisation and Spatial ReasoningA Qwen3-based localisation agent that selects candidate positions using BLE, motion and map evidence, with independent verification and deterministic fallback.ResearchCardiac Segmentation and Spatiotemporal ModellingA study of temporal recalibration and cyclic trajectory regularisation for 2D+t cine MRI segmentation under ED/ES-only supervision.ResearchHydrological Constraints on Post-Fire Vegetation RecoveryAn analysis of modelled soil water and MODIS NDVI using the 2019–2020 Gospers Mountain megafire as a natural experiment.
